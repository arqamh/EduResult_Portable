Saved EduResult projects are kept in this folder.

Each file is named after the school, the examinations it contains and the date it was saved,
for example: "School name - OL 2025 & AL 2024-2025 - 2026-10-11.eduresult.json".

The files are encrypted. They can only be opened in EduResult by someone who can sign in.
To make a backup, copy the whole EduResult_Portable folder (including School and Projects).

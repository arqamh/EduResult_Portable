1. Starting EduResult

EduResult works inside Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge. You do not need the internet, and nothing needs to be installed.

  1. Copy the EduResult_Portable folder to the computer, for example to the Desktop or a USB drive. If you received a ZIP file, right-click it, choose Extract All…, then click Extract.
  2. Open the folder and double-click index.html. If it opens in another program, right-click it, choose Open with, then choose Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge.

The folder contains:

EduResult_Portable ├── index.html ← double-click to start ├── User Guide.html ← this guide ├── School ← your school details, logo, header picture and user accounts ├── Projects ← your saved projects ├── Templates ← empty Excel templates for entering results └── Sample Data ← made-up example files to practise with

2. The first time: create the administrator

  1. Enter your full name, choose a username and a password of at least 8 characters, then click Create administrator.
  2. EduResult shows a recovery key, a code like ABCD-EFGH-…. Write it down or click Print recovery key, and keep it somewhere safe, away from the computer.
  3. Tick I have written down or printed the recovery key and click Save accounts and continue.
  4. Your browser asks you to choose a folder. Choose the EduResult_Portable folder (the one that contains index.html) and click Select Folder. If the browser asks for permission to save changes, click Allow or Save changes.
Why the recovery key matters: saved projects are locked with your accounts. If every administrator forgets their password, the recovery key is the only way to open them again.

3. Signing in and out

  • Each time you open EduResult, sign in with your username and password.
  • Click Log out (top right) when you finish, especially on a shared computer.
  • If nobody uses EduResult for 20 minutes, the screen locks. Enter your password to continue where you were.

4. School details and report header

Open Settings & rules. The School details card holds the school name, address, telephone, email, website and the “Prepared by” name for reports.

  • Report header image: upload your school's letterhead or banner (a wide PNG or JPG picture, about 2000 × 300 pixels). Printed reports then start with this picture.
  • School logo: a small square picture shown in the menu, and on reports when there is no header image.
  • Address, telephone, email and website are printed on reports only if you fill them in.
  • Click Save as school defaults (administrators only). The details are kept in the School folder and filled in automatically every time you start.

The details are stored in School › school-config.js. You can also open that file with Notepad and change the text between the quotes.

5. Importing a results file

  1. Choose O/L or A/L at the top of the menu, then click Import results.
  2. Enter the examination year. Then drag the Excel file onto the box, or click the box to choose it.
  3. EduResult recognises the file type by itself, even if the file name ends in “.xls” while the file is really a newer Excel file.
  4. Check the column list. Each column has been given a meaning (candidate name, index number, subject result and so on). Change any that are wrong.
  5. Click Validate data. EduResult checks every row and lists any problem with its row number. You can download this list as an Excel file.
  6. When there are no errors, click Import. If some rows have errors, you can fix the file and try again, or choose Import only rows without errors.
Official results sheets: cells such as 32TC 3 are read as subject code 32, medium T, grade C. The last number is kept exactly as it is in the file. If a sheet has a “Grade Count” column, EduResult checks that it agrees with the grades it read and shows Grade Count verified.

6. Dashboard, subjects and candidates

  • Dashboard: summary figures, charts and tables. Use the filters (gender, medium, attempt or stream, class, subject, grade) to look at a group. Every figure on the page follows the filters. Click Reset filters to see everyone again.
  • Subjects: grade counts and pass rates for every subject, and the list of candidates who did not pass a chosen subject.
  • Candidates: search by name or index number. Click a candidate to see all their results.
  • Year comparison: compare different years. Import each year's results first.

7. Printing reports and Excel downloads

Open Reports. Each report has two buttons:

  • Print / PDF opens the print window. Choose a printer, or choose Save as PDF to make a PDF file.
  • Excel downloads the same figures as an Excel workbook.

Reports use the results and filters you are currently looking at. They show your header image (or logo and school name), any contact details you filled in, the date, page numbers and an explanation of how each percentage is calculated. For an individual student report, first choose the student on the Candidates page.

8. Saving and opening projects

A project is everything you have imported, plus your settings. Click Save project (top right) to keep it.

  • Projects are saved in the Projects folder with a name that tells you what is inside, for example:
    KL Naleem Hajiar Ladies College - OL 2025 & AL 2024-2025 - 2026-10-11.eduresult.json
  • Saving the same project again on the same day updates the same file.
  • To open a project, go to Project & data. The Saved projects list shows the school, the results inside and when it was saved. Click Open.
  • Saved projects are locked (encrypted). They can only be opened in this EduResult folder by someone who can sign in.
  • “Unsaved changes” at the top means something has not been saved yet.
Backups: copy the whole EduResult_Portable folder to a USB drive or another computer from time to time. Always keep the School and Projects folders together: the projects can only be opened with the accounts in the School folder.

9. Users and passwords

RoleCan do
AdministratorEverything, including adding and removing users, resetting passwords and saving the school defaults.
StaffImport results, view the analysis, print reports, and save and open projects. Can change their own password.
  • Open Users & password to change your own password.
  • Administrators add users there (full name, username, role and a starting password) and can reset anyone's password.
  • Forgot your password? Ask an administrator to reset it. If no administrator can sign in, click Forgot password? Use the recovery key on the sign-in screen.

10. Rules and settings

  • Pass and credit grades: by default, A, B, C and S are passes, and A, B and C are credit passes.
  • O/L qualification rule and A/L eligibility rule: EduResult comes with an example rule marked “not verified”. Enter the rule from the official circular, write where it comes from, and tick “I have verified this rule”. Reports say whether the rule was verified.
  • Special statuses: symbols such as “+” that appear instead of a grade. They are never counted as a failure. Give them a description from your official documents.
  • Subject names: give each subject code its proper name, for example for “1st Subject Group · 61”.

11. What the numbers mean

TermMeaning
Sat a subjectThe candidate has a grade (A, B, C, S or W for O/L; A, B, C, S or F for A/L) for that subject.
Subject pass rateCandidates who passed the subject, out of those who sat it.
Result pass rateAll passing grades, out of all grades given.
All-AEvery grade is A, with at least the full number of subjects.
Special status (e.g. “+”)Not a grade. Shown separately and not counted in any percentage.
Blank cellNo entry for that subject. It is not counted as absent or failed.
Z-Score, district and island rankShown only when they are in the imported file. EduResult never calculates them.

12. Problems and answers

The page looks plain or broken, or says it “could not load its files”

The folder was not extracted from the ZIP. Close the page, extract the ZIP (right-click, Extract All…) and open index.html from the extracted folder.

The browser asks me to choose a folder

Choose the EduResult_Portable folder, the one that contains index.html, not the Projects or School folder inside it. You only need to do this once per computer. The browser may sometimes ask you to allow access again; click Allow.

“This project was saved with different EduResult user accounts”

The project was saved in a different EduResult folder that has its own accounts. Open it in the folder where it was created, or copy that folder's School folder along with it.

Is my students' information safe?

EduResult never sends anything over the internet. Saved projects are encrypted. The Excel files you import and the reports you print or download are ordinary files, so store them carefully and delete copies you no longer need.
